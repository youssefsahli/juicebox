Add game feel to Godot 4
in about 30 seconds.

No complex setups or inheritance chains. Just drop a node into your scene and call play(). It's free, open-source, and strictly GDScript.

Get Started

01. JuiceCamera2D

Trauma-based Perlin shake. Feels heavy, not floaty.

02. ImpactFrame

Time-scale manipulation for hitstop and flashes.

03. SquashStretch

Procedural scale deformation for your sprites.

04. GhostTrail

Spawn independent sprite clones for speed effects.
